Score split: earnings yield rank return on capital rank

Ranking follows Joel Greenblatt's formula. Earnings yield is EBIT divided by enterprise value (market cap plus debt, less cash). Return on capital is EBIT divided by net working capital plus net fixed assets. Each stock is ranked on both, and the lowest combined score ranks first. Ranks are recalculated whenever you change the filters, because they are relative to the stocks in the list.

Loss-making, pre-revenue and foreign-domiciled companies are removed before ranking. EBIT uses trailing twelve months where Yahoo Finance has it, otherwise the last full year (shown as TTM or FY in the period column). Figures reported in other currencies are converted to A$.
